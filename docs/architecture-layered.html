SecOps Lab – Layered Architecture

High-level view of how OpenClaw, agents, MCPs, and external feeds work together
Layer 1
OpenClaw Gateway & Agentic Framework
The foundational platform everything runs on. OpenClaw provides a local gateway, session management, and the agentic framework that powers Jarvis and all SecOps agents. The TUI / CLI is the control surface to start, inspect, and manage agents and their cron schedules.
Core Platform Local Only
OpenClaw Gateway
Sessions · Cron · Tools · TUI/CLI
Manages isolated reasoning sessions, cron jobs, and tools (file access, web, MCPs). The TUI/CLI is how you inspect status, debug agents, and see what the system is doing under the hood.
Orchestrator
Jarvis
Your SecOps copilot
Frontline assistant that lives on top of the gateway: coordinates agents, reads dashboards and artifacts, and turns raw outputs into human-friendly guidance and decisions.
Layer 2
Domain Agents
Specialized reasoning agents focused on different parts of the SecOps story. Each one consumes telemetry and intelligence, then writes structured artifacts and narratives the SOC can act on.
Alerts
Security Analyst
30m alert triage
Continuously pulls alerts from SentinelOne Purple and surrounding telemetry, prioritizes them by risk, and produces clear “act-first” guidance for suspected ransomware, RCE, and identity abuse.
Threat Intel
TI Digest
Daily TI overview
Aggregates external threat intelligence (KEV, campaigns, IOCs), filters it for what matters to this environment, and curates a daily digest that hunts and SOC leadership can pivot from.
Vulnerabilities
Vulnerability Manager
Daily risk posture
Tracks vulnerabilities across assets, correlates them with KEV and exploitation-in-the-wild, and outputs prioritized remediation plans instead of just a wall of CVEs.
Executive View
SOC Manager
Daily & weekly summaries
Pulls together alerts, TI, vulns, and hunts into daily and weekly reports that a CISO or SOC manager can actually read: trends, priorities, and where the coverage gaps are.
Hunting
Threat Hunter
Environment-driven hunts
Uses SentinelOne Purple to hunt for follow-on activity and lateral movement based on what alerts have already seen: credential theft, movement, and post-exploitation behavior in this environment.
Proactive Hunting
Proactive Threat Hunter
TI-driven hunts
Takes curated TI IOCs from the TI Digest and looks for evidence of those campaigns in SentinelOne data, even when they haven’t triggered visible alerts yet.
Hygiene
Janitor
Artifact cleanup
Periodically cleans up old cron artifacts according to retention policy (60d alerts, 90d daily outputs), keeping disk usage under control without impacting dashboards or investigations.
Layer 3
MCP Servers (Tooling Backplane)
Deterministic backends that the agents call for facts: alert streams, hunting capabilities, and threat enrichment. They don’t make decisions; they surface data and actions the agents reason about.
SentinelOne
SentinelOne Purple MCP
Alerts · PowerQuery · Purple AI
Exposes SentinelOne data and hunt capabilities as tools: list_alerts, get_alert, powerquery, purple_ai, and more. Security Analyst and the hunters lean on this to see what endpoints are actually doing.
Threat Intel
TI Aggregator MCP
Enrichment & IOC curation
Normalizes and enriches external IOCs from multiple feeds. Provides tools like enrich_indicator, bulk_enrich, cve_report, and feed snapshot helpers that TI Digest, Vulnerability Manager, and Proactive Hunter build on.
Layer 4
External Data Feeds
The raw material that flows into the MCPs and agents: production telemetry from your environment and curated intelligence from outside. The rest of the stack exists to turn this into decisions and actions.
EDR / XDR
SentinelOne Platform
Endpoint & cloud telemetry
Provides alerts, incident timelines, and huntable telemetry via SentinelOne Purple. This is where ransomware heuristics, lateral movement, and identity abuse detections originate.
Threat Feeds
Multi-source TI
KEV, OTX, GreyNoise, VT, abuse.ch, URLhaus
External indicators and vulnerability intelligence that TI Aggregator pulls together. TI Digest and hunters use this to understand which campaigns, CVEs, and IOCs matter most here.
Skill Security
ClawSec Advisory Feed
Advisories for OpenClaw skills
Security advisories for OpenClaw skills and integrations. Used to flag malicious or vulnerable skills and drive guarded install/remove flows so the platform itself stays trustworthy.
